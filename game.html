Bonus game

Colour Escape

Two to four players, one graph. Pick a colour, pick a starting vertex, then walk across the graph painting as you go. Your colour may never touch itself. Run out of moves and you are trapped.

Set up the game

How to play

  • Start: each player picks a colour, then takes turns tapping a free vertex to start on. That vertex is painted.
  • Your turn: your token walks along edges, passing only through unpainted vertices, and lands on a glowing vertex. That vertex is painted your colour.
  • The colouring rule: no two joined vertices may share a colour. So you can never land next to a vertex of your own colour. Other colours can sit next to yours.
  • Trapped: when no glowing vertex is left for you, you are out. The first player trapped is the loser.
  • Winning: the game ends when everyone is trapped. The player who painted the most vertices dominated the graph and wins.
  • Think graph theory: your colour must form a set of vertices with no edges between them. Use walls of other colours to trap your rivals, and keep your own escape routes open.
