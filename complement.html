The Complement of a Graph

What the numbers say

    Facts to test

    1. G and Ḡ together make the complete graph Kₙ, so |E(G)| + |E(Ḡ)| = n(n−1)/2.

    2. Every vertex has degree d in G and n−1−d in Ḡ, so the "Total" column is always n−1.

    3. The complement of a complete graph has no edges at all. Load Kₙ and check.

    4. If two graphs are isomorphic, their complements are isomorphic too, so comparing complements is another way to test two graphs. Use "Copy complement" and paste the results into the Isomorphism page.
