Isomorphism Checker

Two graphs are isomorphic when you can rename the vertices of one so that it becomes exactly the other. Draw two graphs and find out.

How the checker thinks

First it compares quick checks: the number of vertices, the number of edges, the degrees of the vertices, and the cyclic subgraphs (here the triangles, quadrilaterals and pentagons). If any of these differ, the graphs cannot be isomorphic.

If they all match, that is not proof. It then tries to pair up vertices one by one, keeping only pairings where every edge and non-edge agrees, and backs up when it gets stuck.

The animation uses the pairing it finds. Each vertex of B glides onto the vertex of A it is matched with, and once it arrives it takes that vertex's number. When every vertex has moved, the two drawings are identical, which is exactly what isomorphic means.
