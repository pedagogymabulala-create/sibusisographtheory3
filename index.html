WELCOME TO GRAPH THEORY

Where dots meet lines and every connection tells a story. Draw graphs, count their vertices and degrees, test whether two graphs are secretly the same, and explore complements and Pascal's triangle.

Start exploring Jump to the playground

Three words to begin

Everything in this course is built from these ideas.

Vertex

A point in the graph. Think of a person, a city or a classroom.

Edge

A line joining two vertices. It says "these two are joined".

Degree

How many edges touch a vertex. Add every degree and you get twice the number of edges.
