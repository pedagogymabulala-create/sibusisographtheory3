    Why the triangle belongs in graph theory

    Start counting rows and entries from 0. The first row is row 0, and the first number in each row is entry 0. Each number is the two numbers above it added together.

    Here is the link to graphs. In the complete graph Kₙ, the number of triangles (3-cycles) is entry 3 of row n. Kₙ also has n vertices, each of degree n−1, so by the Sum of Degrees Theorem it has n(n−1)/2 edges.
