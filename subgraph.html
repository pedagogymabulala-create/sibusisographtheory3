Words to know

Subgraph: if graph B is made entirely of vertices and edges that come from a larger graph A, then B is a subgraph of A. You get B by deleting some vertices and edges from A. Every edge of B must still have a vertex at each end.

Not a subgraph: B is not a subgraph of A if it has a vertex or an edge that A does not have.

The "same-number check" in the table above tests exactly this, using the vertex numbers as they are. The search goes one step further. It looks for a copy of H inside G after renaming the vertices, by matching each vertex of H to a different vertex of G so that every edge of H lands on an edge of G. That matching is called a correspondence.
